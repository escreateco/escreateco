📸 Emre Salih — Founder

Emre Salih

Founder & Creative Director

From the age of 5, Emre's drive has always been simple: look after the people around him. That instinct — to care, to show up, to make things better — is what eventually led him to build ES Create Co.

With a Bachelor of Information Technology and 8 years of hands-on photography experience, Emre brings a rare combination of technical precision and creative vision to every project. He's not just building a business — he's channelling a lifelong passion for technology and storytelling into something that genuinely helps people.

At 21, Emre founded ES Create Co. to give brands the premium creative support they deserve — the kind he would have wanted for the people he's always cared about most.
